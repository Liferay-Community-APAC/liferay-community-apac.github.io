Getting Started

Liferay Forums adds a complete forum site to Liferay DXP. This page gets you from installing it to a forum members can use.

On this page

  • Install
  • First-Time Setup
  • Related Topics

Install#

Liferay Forums 1.0.0 runs on Liferay DXP 2026.Q1 LTS with the required Liferay fixes. Installing lists what it includes, and walks you through installing it on Liferay Self-Hosted (Tomcat or Docker), on Liferay PaaS, or on Liferay SaaS. When it finishes, the forum is available at /web/forums.

First-Time Setup#

After installing, complete these steps as an administrator:

  1. Create categories. Open Forum Categories Admin and click Seed Default Categories, or add your own. Members can't post until at least one category exists. See Managing Categories.
  2. Set up email notifications. Set the sender address of the forum's email templates and of your Liferay instance, then restart the microservice, which reads the templates only when it starts. See Email Notifications.
  3. Assign moderators. Give the Forum Moderator role to the people who will moderate, and Forum Category Admin to the people who will manage categories. See Roles and Permissions.
  4. Post a welcome topic. Use New Discussion to post community guidelines, and give it the Announcement priority so it stays at the top.
