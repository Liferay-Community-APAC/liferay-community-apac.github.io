How we work

Five ways we help you succeed with Liferay

The Liferay Customer Success team for Asia Pacific supports you at every stage of your Liferay journey.

  1. 01

    Onboarding & adoption

    We help you get value from Liferay quickly. That means setting clear goals at kick-off, building a success plan around them, and supporting your teams as they adopt the platform.

  2. 02

    Technical advisory & health checks

    We review your architecture, configuration, performance and customizations against Liferay best practices, then give you a clear, prioritized plan for what to improve.

  3. 03

    Upgrades, migrations & lifecycle

    We help you keep up with Liferay's quarterly releases. We plan upgrades, migrate content from legacy platforms, and keep you on a supported, secure version.

  4. 04

    Enablement & knowledge sharing

    We share what we learn from projects across the region as hands-on guidance, sample code and reusable tooling, so your own team can build with confidence.

  5. 05

    Partnership & measurable value

    We stay with you for the long term. Regular business reviews keep track of the outcomes that matter to you, and we connect you with the right Liferay experts across the region whenever you need them.
